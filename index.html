Policy RAG Agent

A corrective RAG agent that answers employee questions from company policy. It cites every answer and abstains when the documents are silent.

A LangGraph loop retrieves, grades relevance, rewrites the query once, generates, then checks that every citation points to retrieved text. Vendor-supplied content is treated as untrusted and scrubbed of instructions aimed at AI systems. Traced and evaluated with MLflow 3, with safety scorers that must stay at 100% in CI.

Commercial Analytics Agent

Plain-English questions over pharma commercial data, answered without the model ever writing SQL.

The agent turns a question into a structured query over a semantic layer of 6 metrics and 9 dimensions defined in YAML. A deterministic compiler builds the SQL, and a sqlglot guard allows only a single SELECT on allowlisted tables. Undefined terms such as profit get a refusal instead of an invented number. Compiled SQL transpiles to Snowflake, Databricks or BigQuery.

Supply Chain Exception Agent

An operations agent on top of a dbt medallion pipeline. It triages late, stuck and damaged shipments against the ops playbook.

The pipeline has 8 dbt models and 15 data tests, and runs on DuckDB locally or Databricks by switching the target. The agent gathers context with read-only tools and proposes actions from a fixed catalog. A policy layer prices each one and applies approval rules: expedites over $500 and messages to strategic accounts wait for a person. Ops leaders get a daily digest.
